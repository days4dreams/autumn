## Staying at Autumn House

### What time is check-in and check-out?
show on: home, enquire
Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. If you'd like to arrive earlier or leave later, just ask and we'll do our best.

### How do we get in?
show on: home
Autumn House has self check-in with a keybox by the front door. We'll send you the code and everything else you need before you arrive.

### Is there parking?
show on: home, house
Yes, free on-street parking right outside the front door.

### Is there Wi-Fi?
show on: house
Yes, free Wi-Fi throughout the house. You'll find the details inside when you arrive.

### Can we bring the dog?
show on: house, family
Sorry, we don't normally allow pets, but [drop us a message](/enquire) and we'll see what we can do.

### Can we smoke or vape?
Autumn House is non-smoking inside, but you're welcome to use the front or back garden.

### Where can we buy food?
show on: house
There's a handy, well-stocked corner shop less than a minute's walk from the front door, so rather than leave milk that might go to waste, we let you choose your favourite there. Sainsbury's, M&S and Turnbulls are a three-minute drive away at Willowburn Retail Park, and there's an Aldi on South Road. Our [guide to eating out](/the-guide/where-to-eat-in-alnwick) has our favourite cafés, pubs and restaurants.

## The House

### How many people can stay?
show on: house, family
Up to four guests: the main bedroom has a double bed and the second bedroom has two single beds. We ask that no more than four people stay at any one time.

### What's in the kitchen?
show on: house
An induction hob, oven, microwave, kettle and an integrated washer-dryer, plus pots, pans, crockery, cutlery and children's dinnerware.

### How is the house heated?
show on: house
All the heating is electric and warms up quickly. Each room has its own panel heater, the kitchen and bathroom have underfloor heating, and there's a heated towel rail in the bathroom. The living room also has an electric stove for a cosy glow.

### Is there a garden?
show on: house, family
A small front garden with a picket fence, and a paved back yard with potted plants and chairs for a drink in the sun.

### Is there anything to do on a rainy day?
show on: family
Plenty. There's a projector for streaming films and shows, board games, books and a Bluetooth speaker in the living room.

### Where is the bathroom?
show on: house, family
The bathroom is on the ground floor, and both bedrooms are upstairs. It's an old cottage, so the stairs can be a little creaky at night.

## Families

### Do you have a travel cot and highchair?
show on: family
Yes. There's a travel cot with a mattress and sheets, a highchair with a tray, and a baby bath.

### Is Autumn House good for children?
show on: family
We think so. There's children's tableware, books and toys for every age from babies to ten-plus, board games, a bath for bedtime, and the castle, The Alnwick Garden and Barter Books are all within walking distance.

### What is there for children nearby?
show on: family
Willowburn Leisure Centre has swimming just round the corner, The Alnwick Playhouse is an independent cinema, and Barter Books has a children's area. Hulne Park is lovely for family walks (check its opening times first), and Alnmouth's beach is a short drive away.

## Booking

### Why book direct?
show on: home, enquire
Booking directly with us is our best rate: there are no platform fees, and you deal with us from start to finish. Stayed before? Ask about our returning guest discount.

### How do payment and cancellation work?
show on: enquire
When you book direct, we take 50% when you book and the rest 21 days before you arrive. Cancel more than 21 days before your stay at no cost; after that, we keep the 50% paid at booking. Our [terms](/terms) have the full details.

### Is there a security deposit?
show on: enquire
We place a £125 hold on your card, which is released once we've checked everything is in order after you leave.

### Do you offer discounts for longer stays?
show on: enquire
Yes. When you book direct, weekly stays are 15% off and monthly stays are 25% off.

### Can we add a hamper?
show on: home, enquire, family
Yes. We offer welcome hampers for families, celebrations, walkers and more. They need to be booked at least 7 days before you arrive. [See the hampers](/the-guide/welcome-hampers).
