Skip the dash to the shops on arrival day. Our hampers are waiting when you arrive, so your break can start the moment you walk through the door. Hampers need to be booked at least 7 days before you arrive: [get in touch](/enquire) to add one to your stay.

## The Family *Adventure* · £48

Everything for a good start: cereal, milk, bread, honey, fruit and orange juice for breakfast, a craft kit and colouring book for the children, a local walks guide, snacks, biscuits and hot chocolate for cosy evenings.

## Romance *&* Celebration · £65

A bottle of prosecco or wine, two chilled beers, chocolates and fresh flowers, with cheese, crackers, crisps and chutney for the evening, and granola, honey, fruit and milk for the morning after. Perfect for anniversaries, proposals and couples treating themselves.

## Birthday *Bash* · £40

Your choice of prosecco, wine, beers or very special soft drinks, cupcakes or a cutting cake with candles, balloons and decorations, napkins, crisps and nuts. We'll leave a birthday banner and party hat waiting for the lucky guest.

## Luxury *Northumberland* · £85

The very best of the county: premium local gin, beers or wine, local cheeses, artisan crackers, luxury chocolates, fresh flowers, premium coffee and local honey, with granola, yogurt, eggs, juice, bread and milk for breakfast, and a keepsake from Alnwick to take home.

## The Walker's *Retreat* · £36

Fuel for the trails: energy bars, trail mix, flapjacks, fruit, honey, fresh bread, milk and orange juice, a copy of Living North, and hot chocolate and a muscle soak for when you get back.
